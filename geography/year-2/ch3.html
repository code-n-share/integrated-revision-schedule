⚙️ Case Study 1: Aughinish Alumina (Heavy Industry)
RUSAL Aughinish Alumina is a factory on Aughinish Island in the Shannon estuary (where the river meets the sea). RUSAL is the world’s largest aluminium provider.
💡 It’s a “halfway” factory: it turns heavy rock (bauxite) into a white powder (alumina), which other factories turn into aluminium.
Location factorHow it applies to Aughinish
Raw materialsBauxite is bulky and heavy: shipped straight to the deep-water port at Aughinish; the island can store large quantities.
TransportPort at Aughinish; the N69 road links to Shannon Airport.
MarketEuropean market: Ireland is an EU country, so alumina can be easily shipped to European smelters (in the UK).
Labour450 people from nearby towns, e.g. Foynes and Askeaton.
⚠️ Common mistake: saying Aughinish makes aluminium. It makes alumina (a semi-finished white powder) which is exported to be made into aluminium elsewhere.
💻 Case Study 2: Apple, Cork (Light Industry)
Apple Computers Ltd is a light industry in Holyhill, Cork. It produces information technology (IT) products, e.g. computers, iPods and iPhones, for the world market, and employs 6,000 workers in the Cork City area.

It came to Cork in the 1980s and has grown due to increasing demand. It is their operational headquarters for Europe, the Middle East and Asia.

🌐 Multinational Corporations (MNCs) and Google
MNCs are corporations making their products in many different countries. The headquarters are in one country and manage branches in other countries. Because of globalisation it’s easier to buy and sell across the world.
💡 Think of a school with one head office and branch classrooms in many towns: the head office makes the big decisions.

Google in Ireland: set up in Ireland in 2003, providing 100 jobs; it now employs 2,500 people. 1 in 5 people are now employed by an MNC.

Reasons why MNCs such as Google locate in Ireland:

  • Ireland is an EU country
  • Access to markets and labour force
  • A highly educated workforce
  • English is the language of business
  • Corporation tax is low (12.5%)
🧩 Remember it: E-M-E-E-T = EU, Markets & labour, Educated workforce, English language, low Tax.

MNCs locating a base in Ireland is called foreign direct investment (FDI). Downside: if an MNC leaves, it causes job losses.

⚔️ Industry and Conflict
There can be conflict between factory owners and residents: gas and smoke emissions could harm health and the environment. Example: incinerators in Poolbeg, Co. Dublin. Incinerators burn waste.

✅ Positives

  • Save space on landfills
  • Provide electricity
  • Jobs created to build and maintain it

❌ Negatives

  • An easy option, so recycling may not be tried
  • Complaints that fumes are carcinogenic (cancer-causing)
  • Smoke can contribute to smog, acid rain and greenhouse gases
⚠️ Common mistake: giving only one side. For a “discuss” question give positives and negatives, and name the example (Poolbeg incinerator).

✏️ Practice Questions

Written from your class notes. Click a question to reveal a model answer.

1. Case study: Aughinish Alumina. Describe its inputs, processes and outputs.

Inputs: bauxite and caustic soda. Processes: bauxite is crushed, mixed and heated. Outputs: alumina powder and waste.

2. Explain why Aughinish Alumina is located on Aughinish Island.

Bauxite is a bulky, heavy raw material, shipped to the deep-water port at Aughinish, and the island can store large quantities of it. Ireland is in the EU, so alumina can be easily shipped to European smelters. Transport includes the port and the N69 road to Shannon Airport, and 450 workers come from nearby towns such as Foynes and Askeaton.

3. Case study: Apple. Give three reasons why it located in Cork.

Low corporation tax of 12.5%. Free movement of goods, since Ireland is an EU country. Close to Port of Cork and Cork Airport, so goods can be shipped worldwide at low cost. Also close to UCC and CIT, which supply skilled graduates.

4. What is an MNC? Use a named example in Ireland.

A multinational corporation makes its products in many different countries. Its headquarters are in one country and manage branches in other countries. Example: Google, set up in Ireland in 2003 with 100 jobs and now employing 2,500 people.

5. Explain why MNCs locate in Ireland, and one downside of this.

Ireland is an EU country with access to markets and a labour force, a highly educated workforce, English as the language of business and a low corporation tax (12.5%). This is foreign direct investment (FDI). Downside: if the MNC leaves, there are job losses.

6. Discuss the conflict that can arise over an incinerator, e.g. Poolbeg, Co. Dublin.

Conflict between factory owners and residents: gas and smoke emissions could harm health and the environment. Positives: saves space on landfills, provides electricity, creates jobs to build and maintain it. Negatives: an easy option so recycling may not be tried; complaints that fumes are carcinogenic; smoke can contribute to smog, acid rain and greenhouse gases.
