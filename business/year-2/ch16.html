🧩 Remember it: Financial = For profit · Social = Society first · Cultural = Creative arts.
⚠️ Common mistake: saying a social enterprise makes no money. The point is that people and community come ahead of private gain – always say who benefits.
💶 Financial Enterprise
Role of financial enterprises in society
  • Provide goods and services.
  • Create employment – direct and indirect.
  • Pay taxes.
  • Offer finance – e.g. banks, building societies, credit unions, An Post.
  • Economic growth – an increase in the amount of goods and services produced in an economy from one year to the next.
Support for financial enterprises
  • There are 31 LEOs (Local Enterprise Offices) across the country that offer people a range of support and services to people interested in setting up a business, e.g. financial advice, mentoring, business information, advice and training.
  • Enterprise Ireland: a government agency that helps businesses who want to expand abroad – financial support, assistance with research and development, export assistance.
⚠️ Common mistake: mixing up the two supports. LEO = help to set up a business locally; Enterprise Ireland = help to expand abroad.
🤝 Social Enterprise
Role of social enterprises
RoleWhat it means
Tackle social problemsRaise awareness of and try to tackle social issues such as homelessness and unemployment.
Provide servicesSupport for communities that are not served by state or commercial enterprises.
EmploymentHelp to create jobs in local communities.
FinanceSome social enterprises, such as SEI and Clann Credo, provide finance for other social enterprises.
Enterprise cultureHelp to create a culture of entrepreneurship and self-help.
Support for social enterprises
  • Social Entrepreneurs Ireland (SEI): not-for-profit organisation that helps social entrepreneurs by providing financial and practical support.
  • Clann Credo: social investment fund that provides loans to community organisations, charities and social enterprises.
📘 Case study: FoodCloud (from your worksheet)
  • FoodCloud is a social enterprise that connects businesses that have too much food and don't want it to go to waste (with charities).
  • Around 30% of food produced is wasted globally.
  • Reasons food is discarded: blemished or misshapen; mechanical damage.
  • FoodCloud now works with 2,500 retail stores.
  • Evaluation: a worthwhile enterprise, because the food goes to people who can't afford it, e.g. meals on wheels.

✏️ Practice Questions
Distinguish between a financial, a social and a cultural enterprise, giving an example of each.
Financial: set up to make a profit, provides goods and services (supermarket, bank, hairdresser). Social: puts people and community ahead of private and personal gain (charity, park, playground). Cultural: provides access to visual arts, films, music, radio, literature (museum, gallery).
Outline four roles of financial enterprises in society.
Provide goods and services; create employment (direct and indirect); pay taxes; offer finance (banks, building societies, credit unions, An Post); contribute to economic growth (increase in goods and services produced from one year to the next).
Name two supports available to a person setting up a financial enterprise and describe each.
Local Enterprise Offices (31 across the country): financial advice, mentoring, business information, advice and training. Enterprise Ireland: government agency that helps businesses expand abroad with financial support, R&D assistance and export assistance.
Explain the role of social enterprises in society and name one support for them.
Tackle social problems (e.g. homelessness, unemployment); provide services to communities not served by the state or commercial enterprises; create jobs locally; provide finance for other social enterprises; build an enterprise culture. Supports: Social Entrepreneurs Ireland (financial and practical help) or Clann Credo (loans).
Describe three ways cultural enterprises benefit society and the local economy.
Promote Irish arts and culture (music, sport, literature); create employment; support the local economy through direct jobs, tourism and festivals; entertainment that improves wellbeing. Supported by Cultural Ireland and the Arts Council of Ireland.
FoodCloud is a social enterprise. Evaluate its role in society (suggested answer – check against your textbook).
It connects businesses that have too much food with those who can use it, so less food is wasted (around 30% of food produced is wasted globally) and people who can't afford food benefit (e.g. via meals on wheels). It works with around 2,500 retail stores, so its impact is wide. It puts community ahead of private gain, which fits the definition of a social enterprise.
