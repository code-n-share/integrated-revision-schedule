📣 Marketing and Market Research
Marketing: the process of identifying and satisfying customer wants and needs while making a profit.
💡 Find out what people want, give it to them, and still make money.
Market research: gathering, recording and analysing information about consumer preferences for a good or service in order to make informed decisions about a potential market.
💡 Asking before you launch, so you don't guess.
Benefits of conducting market research
  • Information about customer needs and wants
  • Information on competitors
  • Customer reactions to changes to existing products or services
  • The best prices to charge customers
  • Customer reactions to products being promoted
⚠️ Common mistake: defining market research as just "asking customers questions". The definition has three verbs – gathering, recording and analysing – and a purpose: informed decisions.
🔀 Types of Market Research
Two types of market research: field research (primary) and desk research (secondary).
Market research→Field (primary): surveys · focus group · observation|Desk (secondary): internal · external

🖥️ Desk Research
Desk research (secondary research): looking at and analysing information that has already been gathered for another purpose or from another source. It can be internal or external.
Example sourceWhat it tells you
Business sales reportsHelps identify the most and least successful products
Newspapers and magazinesInformation about competitors or consumers
The internetChecks competitor products and prices; finds information about market size and trends
Central Statistics OfficeInformation on the population of towns and cities including age, gender, marital status, etc.
Desk research – advantages and disadvantages
AdvantagesDisadvantages
• Easy to obtain, as it uses information that's already available
• Quick to gather
• Cheaper than field research, as it doesn't need trained interviewers		 • The information isn't gathered for the business's specific needs, so it may not be as useful or relevant as primary research
• Information may be outdated
• Researcher may have to read lots of information to find what they need
⚠️ Common mistake: calling sales reports "external". A business's own sales reports are internal desk research (inside the business); newspapers, the internet and the CSO come from outside.

Which statement best defines market research?

  1. Selling goods at a profit
  2. Gathering, recording and analysing information about consumer preferences to make informed decisions
  3. Advertising a new product
  4. Setting the price of a product
Market research gathers, records and analyses information on consumer preferences to support informed decisions about a potential market.

Which is a benefit of market research?

  1. It guarantees profit
  2. It tells the business the best prices to charge customers
  3. It replaces the need for advertising
  4. It removes competitors
Benefits include information on customers' needs and wants, competitors, reactions to changes, best prices and reactions to promotion. Nothing guarantees profit.

A business conducts a survey of its customers. This is…

  1. Desk research
  2. Secondary research
  3. Field research
  4. Internal research
Going out to gather first-hand information (surveys, focus groups, observation) is field/primary research.

A company reads last year's industry report to learn about market trends. This is…

  1. Field research
  2. Primary research
  3. Observation
  4. Desk research
Information already gathered by someone else is desk (secondary) research.

Which is a "yes/no" survey question?

  1. Open-ended
  2. Closed
  3. Leading
  4. Multiple response
Closed questions have a yes/no answer. Open-ended questions let customers say what they like.

Why should survey questions NOT be leading?

  1. They take too long
  2. They push the customer towards an answer, making results unreliable
  3. They cost more
  4. They need trained interviewers
Leading questions steer the answer; questions should be clear and not leading.

Which is an advantage of desk research?

  1. It is gathered for the business's exact needs
  2. It is always up to date
  3. It is quick to gather and cheaper than field research
  4. It involves direct contact with customers
Desk research is easy, quick and cheaper (no trained interviewers). Its drawbacks: may be irrelevant or outdated.

Which is a disadvantage of desk research?

  1. It is expensive
  2. The information may be outdated
  3. It needs trained interviewers
  4. It is slow to obtain
Information may be outdated (and may not match the business's specific needs). The others are false for desk research.

Which of these is an example of desk research?

  1. Observing shoppers in a store
  2. Running a focus group
  3. Using Central Statistics Office population data
  4. Asking customers to fill in a questionnaire
CSO data was gathered by someone else already, so it is desk research. The other three are field research.
✏️ Practice Questions
Define marketing and market research.
Marketing: the process of identifying and satisfying customer wants and needs while making a profit. Market research: gathering, recording and analysing information about consumer preferences for a good or service in order to make informed decisions about a potential market.
Outline three benefits to a business of conducting market research.
Any three: information on customers' needs and wants; information on competitors; customer reactions to changes to existing products or services; the best prices to charge; customer reactions to products being promoted.
Distinguish between field research and desk research, giving examples of each.
Field (primary) research: going out to the marketplace to get information first-hand for a specific purpose, by direct contact with potential and existing customers – e.g. surveys, focus groups, observation. Desk (secondary) research: using information already gathered for another purpose or from another source, internal or external – e.g. sales reports, newspapers and magazines, the internet, Central Statistics Office.
Describe the three types of survey question and give a rule for writing good questions.
Closed: yes/no. Multiple choice: allows several answers (as in your notes). Open-ended: customer says what they like rather than being confined to a specific answer. Questions should be clear and not leading.
Discuss the advantages and disadvantages of desk research.
Advantages: easy to obtain (information already exists); quick to gather; cheaper than field research (no trained interviewers). Disadvantages: not gathered for the business's specific needs so may be less useful or relevant than primary research; information may be outdated; researcher may have to read lots of information to find what they need.
